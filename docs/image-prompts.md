# Site photos — what goes where, and prompts to generate them

The site is designed to look finished without photos, but real photography is
what moves it from "clean" to "established firm". Each slot below falls back
gracefully when empty.

## Two ways to fill a slot

1. **Admin (recommended):** `/admin` → Images → pick the slot → *Generate with
   AI* (uses kie.ai) or upload a photo. Hit Publish.
2. **Repo:** save the file in `public/images/` using the slot name —
   `home_hero.jpg`, `home_dealer.jpg`, `about_portrait.jpg`, `ai_hero.jpg`
   (`.webp`, `.jpg` or `.png`). The admin overrides a file if both are set.

Keep files under ~400 KB (export as WebP or JPG at 80% quality).

## Ground rules for every generated image

- **No text, logos or badges in the image.** Headlines live in the page, where
  Google can read them. AI models also garble lettering and brand badges.
- **No recognisable brand grilles or emblems.** You work with several brands;
  a generic modern vehicle avoids looking like you only serve one — and avoids
  trademark problems.
- **Real-world light, Atlantic Canada feel.** Overcast, golden hour, or dusk.
  Not glossy studio renders.
- **People look like real staff and customers**, not models. Natural poses,
  everyday clothes.

## 1. Homepage hero — `home_hero` (4:3, landscape)

Sits beside the headline on a dark navy background, so a darker, moody image
works best.

> Photorealistic photo of a modern car dealership exterior at dusk in Atlantic
> Canada, late autumn. Rows of clean SUVs and sedans on the lot, wet pavement
> reflecting warm showroom lights through large glass windows. Overcast sky
> with the last bit of blue light. Calm, confident, premium but not flashy.
> Shot on a 35mm lens at eye level, shallow depth of field. No people in
> focus. No text, no logos, no brand emblems on the vehicles. 4:3.

**Alternate (people-focused):**

> Photorealistic photo inside a bright, modern car dealership showroom. A
> salesperson in a navy quarter-zip hands keys to a smiling couple in their
> 40s beside a new crossover SUV. Natural window light, candid moment, not
> posed for the camera. Clean, uncluttered showroom. No text, no logos, no
> brand emblems on the vehicle. 4:3.

## 2. Homepage photo band — `home_dealer` (16:9, wide)

A full-width strip between Services and Free tools. Should feel like a real
day at the store.

> Photorealistic wide shot of a dealership service drive on a fall morning in
> Nova Scotia. A service advisor with a tablet greets a customer next to a
> mid-size SUV pulling into the bay. Soft overcast daylight, a few orange
> leaves on the pavement, clean concrete and glass building. Documentary
> style, candid, natural colours. No text, no logos, no brand emblems. 16:9.

## 3. About portrait — `about_portrait` (4:5, portrait)

**Use a real photo of yourself here — do not generate this one.** Dealers are
hiring you; a generated face undercuts every trust signal on the page.

What to shoot (a phone in portrait mode is fine):

- Standing on a dealership lot or in a showroom, cars soft in the background.
- Waist-up, slightly off-centre, looking at the camera, relaxed smile.
- Navy, grey or white top — matches the site. No busy patterns.
- Overcast daylight or open shade. Avoid harsh midday sun.

A photo here also replaces the quote panel on the homepage's "Why dealers call
me" section.

## 4. AI page hero — `ai_hero` (4:3, landscape)

> Photorealistic close-up of a person's hands holding a smartphone, scrolling
> a vehicle listing page with an SUV photo, sitting on a couch in the evening.
> A car key fob and a coffee mug on the table beside them. Warm lamp light,
> shallow depth of field, cozy and real. Screen content blurred and
> unreadable. No text, no logos. 4:3.

## 5. Guide covers (16:9) — template

Each guide can have a cover (Guides → edit → Cover image). Swap the bracketed
part for the topic:

> Photorealistic documentary-style photo at a car dealership: [SCENE].
> Natural light, Atlantic Canada setting, candid and unposed, muted realistic
> colours. No text, no logos, no brand emblems. 16:9.
